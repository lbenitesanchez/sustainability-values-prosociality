Figure 1. M2 structural regressions and measurement specification. Panel A shows all 21 focal regressions; arrow styles distinguish predictor families, not statistical significance. The four value portraits are separate observed variables, not indicators of an additional latent factor. Panel B shows the measurement specification: SC and SBL have 19 distinct APS-S indicators and the two cross-loadings SC → APS13 and SBL → APS2; PSB is measured by PP1–PP3. Each grouped box abbreviates separate indicators and loadings, not a score or parcel. Seven covariates (Colombia, Peru and Mexico institutional indicators, sex, study level, standardized age and standardized socioeconomic level) enter every equation for the four values, SC, SBL and PSB (49 control regressions, omitted for readability). The six residual covariances among the four values and the SC–SBL disturbance covariance are freely estimated; these are conditional residual covariances, not total factor correlations. Observed residual variances, endogenous disturbances, intercepts and fixed exogenous sample moments are omitted. APS1, APS11 and PP1 are the marker loadings fixed to 1 for SC, SBL and PSB, respectively. The diagram follows the archived M2-SEM estimated with MLR (N = 653), not the model treating PP1–PP3 as three outcomes; complete loading and path estimates are reported in the supplement. Arrows denote cross-sectional regressions and do not establish causality or temporal order.
