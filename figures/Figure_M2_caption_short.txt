Figure 1. M2 structural and measurement specification (MLR, N = 653). Panel A shows the 21 focal regressions; panel B shows measurement, with SC → APS13 and SBL → APS2 as the two cross-loadings. Rectangles denote observed variables and ovals denote latent factors; the four value portraits are separate observed variables. Grouped boxes abbreviate individual indicators, not composites. Seven controls enter each equation for the four values, SC, SBL and PSB: three institutional indicators, sex, study level, standardized age and socioeconomic level. Six residual covariances among values and the SC–SBL disturbance covariance are estimated but omitted; residual variances, intercepts and fixed exogenous moments are also omitted. APS1, APS11 and PP1 are marker loadings fixed to 1. Colors identify arrow families, not significance. Arrows denote cross-sectional associations, not causality or temporal order.
